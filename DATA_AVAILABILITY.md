# Data availability

## Included

- Code for FFT seeding, dispersion-aware calculations, TMM fitting, and
  robustness analysis.
- Derived experiment tables, diagnostics, figures, research notes, and the
  full manuscript source/PDF packages.

## Excluded or restricted

Raw FTIR measurement attachments and any source files governed by their
provider's terms are intentionally not republished unless their provenance
explicitly permits redistribution.  Their expected local location and the
processing sequence are documented in the experiment scripts and project
notes.

The included outputs make the reported analysis inspectable; independent
re-execution from raw spectra requires access to the original measurement
files and the same acquisition metadata.
